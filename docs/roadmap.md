# Roadmap

## Done in v0.1.0

TOTP/HOTP/Steam, LastPass import, otpauth links and QR scan, favorites, folders, drag ordering, brand badges, Keychain storage, Touch ID lock, auto-lock, clipboard clearing, menu bar popover, global shortcut, launch at login, English/Spanish.

## Ideas

- Encrypted export and restore (password-protected).
- Import from Google Authenticator migration QR (`otpauth-migration://`) and other formats.
- Drag-and-drop a QR image onto the window.
- Notarized releases.
- Custom icons per service.

## Out of scope

Cloud sync, accounts, telemetry, iOS/Windows/Linux/web, password management.
